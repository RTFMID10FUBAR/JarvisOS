UNREVIEWED / KEEP

UNREVIEWED — not yet looked at. KEEP — a human confirmed it is real, relevant, and stays.

DUPLICATE / SUPERSEDED

DUPLICATE — a byte-identical or superseded copy of another document. SUPERSEDED — an earlier version of something that was later replaced. Neither removes the document from the record.

IRRELEVANT / ARCHIVE_PROPOSED

IRRELEVANT — not related to any matter. ARCHIVE_PROPOSED — proposed for 99_ARCHIVE, awaiting a move decision a human still has to make. Nothing is moved by setting this status.

Why there is no score

Every number here is a count with a denominator, so it can be checked against the record. Triage does not rate a document's importance and does not predict how it will be used.
