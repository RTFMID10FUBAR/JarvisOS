No access barriers recorded. If a filing was ever prevented or delayed, log it — a contemporaneous entry carries weight that a later reconstruction does not.

How to log one

case-command access log \
  --date 2026-03-10 \
  --type NO_ELECTRICITY \
  --blocked "Response to APCo Answer in PSC 26-0315" \
  --deadline "Response due" --deadline-date 2026-03-15 \
  --tried "Public library printer" \
  --result "Library closed; could not print before deadline" \
  --hours 4 --matter-id 2

--blocked is required and rejects a blank. A general statement of hardship is not evidence; a named filing with a date is.

What makes an entry hold up

A named filing, not a feeling

"The response due March 15" beats "things were hard."

Attached proof

A shutoff notice, a receipt, a failed e-filing confirmation. Without one the entry stays UNKNOWN rather than verified.

Told to the tribunal at the time

A barrier raised for the first time on appeal is much harder to rely on than one the record already shows.
